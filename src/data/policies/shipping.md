At MUSHI, we strive to provide efficient and reliable shipping services to ensure your order arrives in a timely manner. Below, you will find details regarding our shipping methods, processing times, and policies regarding lost or damaged items.

## 1. Shipping Options & Delivery Timeframes
### Free Shipping
- **Method:** Orders are shipped via USPS Ground, with an estimated delivery time of 2-5 business days.
- **Processing Time:** Please allow up to 48 business hours for order processing, which includes packaging and preparation for shipment.
- **Delivery Timeframe:** Your order should arrive within 7 business days from the time of purchase.
### Expedited Shipping
- **Method:** For faster delivery, select Expedited Shipping, which typically arrives within 1-3 business days.
- **Processing Time:** Expedited orders require up to 48 business hours for processing before shipment.
- **Delivery Timeframe:** Your order should arrive within 5 business days from the time of purchase.

**Important Note:** Delivery times begin once your order is in USPS’s possession and not from the time of purchase. Please consider processing time when estimating your order’s arrival.

## 2. Returns & Exchanges
- **Final Sale Policy:** Due to the nature of our products, all consumable items are Final Sale and are not eligible for returns or exchanges.
- **Incorrect or Damaged Items:** If you receive an incorrect or damaged order, please contact our customer support team within 48 hours of delivery to resolve the issue.

## 3. Lost or Damaged Items
- **Shipping Carrier Responsibility:** Once an order is handed over to the carrier, MUSHI is not liable for lost or damaged items during transit.
- **Claims Process:** If your order is lost or arrives damaged, please contact USPS or the shipping carrier directly to file a claim.
- **Package Theft:** We are not responsible for stolen packages once marked as delivered. Please ensure your delivery address is secure.
